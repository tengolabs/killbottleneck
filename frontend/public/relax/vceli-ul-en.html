Click on the meadow. The bees will come for nectar.

Keyboard shortcuts: F nectar, B add bees, K plant a flower, H add a honey harvest to the jar, R free one stuck bee, P pause, M sound, N light, S slow pace, L fireflies. The honeycomb holds 60 drops; any more honey overflows down the trunk and can trap bees. Harvesting stops the overflow; a stuck bee is freed by clicking it or by the Free a bee button. Three successful harvests fill the jar on the left. A bear drinks the full jar and after it leaves you can fill it again. Escape closes the light menu. This is a playful simulation, not a beekeeping guide.
